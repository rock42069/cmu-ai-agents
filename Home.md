# Home

This vault is a growing, curated learning wiki.

## Capture

- Put quick notes, links, and fragments in [[inbox/README|Inbox]].
- Put original documents and saved references in [[raw/README|Raw sources]].
- When a source is worth integrating, ask Codex to ingest it.

## Wiki

- [[wiki/index|Index]] — map of maintained knowledge
- [[wiki/log|Log]] — chronological record of wiki operations

The vault begins deliberately empty. Its structure should emerge from what is
actually useful rather than from assumptions about the course.
